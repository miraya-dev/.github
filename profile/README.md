### 🪞 About Miraya

**Miraya (مرايا)** means *"mirrors"* in Arabic.

We're an independent product studio from Morocco. We design, build and ship real products end-to-end: mobile apps, web platforms, secure backends and AI-powered tools.

**Our principle:** build products people can trust.

---

### 🚀 Products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🛡️ **AMANE** | Community anti-scam platform for the MENA region: check a phone number, bank account or name, and report fraud with proof |
| 📄 **[Sira](https://sira-eta.vercel.app)** | Turns your story into a professional CV and portfolio page, with 30 hand-built designs |

*More products are on the way.*
